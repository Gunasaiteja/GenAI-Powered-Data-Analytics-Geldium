Geldium AI Collections Strategy

A GenAI-powered data analytics job-simulation portfolio exploring delinquency risk, customer outreach, and responsible AI.

About the Project

This project explores how predictive insights can help a financial-services collections team identify potential repayment difficulties and plan timely, appropriate customer support.

Focus areas: EDA concepts, predictive analytics, business recommendations, agentic AI concepts, fairness, explainability, and human oversight.

Proposed System Workflow

1. Inputs → Validate customer and account data.

2. Decision → Estimate risk and review relevant signals.

3. Action → Select an approved reminder or support option.

4. Learning loop → Measure outcomes, review fairness, and validate improvements.
